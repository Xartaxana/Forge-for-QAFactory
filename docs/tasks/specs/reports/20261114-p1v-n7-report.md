   (named error, exit 2). See Q1.

## Choices on edges the texts left open (error direction = never a pass; confirm or change)
- A bar with any request the catalogue cannot price is `unknown` (not the lower-bound sum), count in `unpriced`.
- `release-check` with no `release:` line at all is exit 2 (named), not 0. A lone `release: no previous release` line is 0.
- A `release: owner's word needed` line whose shape is neither the OQ2 form nor `<row> <pair> ...` stays open (exit 1).
- A `bar_changes` entry without a bar, a non-empty word, or a decision of keep|lift is ignored and printed as a `release: note`; a `worsened_passes` entry needs a non-empty word.
- The list is capped at 20 lines then `... and N more` (reuses `scoreboard._named`/`MAX_NAMED`).
- Message ids are de-duplicated per transcript only (as `compare.scan`), the sum over sessions is a plain sum.
- `bar_better_line`: a bar value or factory value that is not a number/range, or a row with no direction, is a named `Refusal`; overlapping ranges give no line (not decided is a `compare --release` matter).
