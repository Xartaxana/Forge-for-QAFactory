## Question (F4) - the invariant cannot hold with five lines

Current final order of `warnings`: [experiment][criteria][sides_not_kept...][implementation-source][readiness WARN lines]
[stand line][data_cleanup lines][S0 pass lines]. Inserting the taken-title line right after the criteria line puts it before
the sides/source lines and before every readiness `WARN`. Gate A shows the first five, so whenever the front already holds
4 lines (e.g. experiment + criteria + source + 1 side) and a WARN was 5th, the WARN becomes 6th - the invariant "a WARN is never
displaced out of the first five by it" and "the title line is within the five" cannot both hold with 5 lines in that case.
Left unimplemented. Order I see as the options: (a) right after the criteria line (title always visible, WARN may drop past
5 in the 4-front-line case); (b) right after the readiness WARN lines (WARN safe, title may drop past 5 - today's position,
only moved ahead of stand/cleanup/other pass lines); (c) queue as an "Ostatki" node. Which one?
