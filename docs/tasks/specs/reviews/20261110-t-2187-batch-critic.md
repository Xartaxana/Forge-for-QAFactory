**M1 (K3, K4; rule 15 pair cross-match): a `person` basis stands on ANY substring of ANY S7 rework words, including words that say "split".**
`PKG/scripts/bug_records.py:1879-1902` (`recorded_person_words`) collects the reason of EVERY S7 dispatch marked `person_words`. It is not limited to the gate B merge decision, to the rework that asked for this merge, or to the draft's cases. `:1969-1971` then accepts the basis when `quote in _fold(words)`, with no minimum length.
Probe (pure, run against the merged tree; output verbatim): recorded words `split 2: these are different bugs, not one bug`, merged draft with 2 manifestations, `basis=[{kind: person, quote: "one bug"}]` -> `problems: []`, `merged_unconfirmed: []`. With `quote: "e"` -> `problems: []`.
So F-2 (a), the only code guard on a merge, is passed by words the person said AGAINST the merge, or by a single letter of any S7 rework words. After that, the packet prints "Cause, confirmed: ..." (`gate_brief.py:4251ff`) and the report prints "One cause (confirmed): ...". That is a confirmed cause printed on evidence that does not stand (FR-41, AR-28).
Condition A (the quote is in some recorded words) and condition B (this draft's merge) are not scoped to one carrier. The existing `person` source of a place answer does scope them: `place_answers._person_list` (`:364-402`) is tied to ONE `--dispatch` and compares the words file to that dispatch's reason.
Fix: the `person` entry names its `dispatch`. It stands only when (a) that dispatch is an S7 rework with `person_words`, (b) the dispatch's scope cases cover the draft's cases (`draft_cases`), and (c) the squashed quote is in that dispatch's reason. Give the shape `person {dispatch, quote}` on the key page (see m3). Whether the words mean "merge" stays the scribe's and the person's question (AR-29). No bug is lost (the split door is shown again), which is why this is a major and not a blocker.
