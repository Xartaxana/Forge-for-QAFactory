LIMITS (stated, not hidden):
- A caller of a reader is one row at the CALL site. Where the line itself names the key (`journal_path`, `cost`, `prompts.slices`,
  `execution.verify_node_ids`) the key is in the row; otherwise the row says "whole dict handed on (keys read downstream, not enumerated at the site)".
  Keys read AFTER the hand-over are the R2 detector's job (node f), not this list's.
- A multi-line read is one row at the line that holds the `read_text`/`read_json` call.
- A read of the config through another module's reader is one row at the call site AND the reader itself has a row (for example
  `grammar.py:293`, `window_budget.py:99`). The reader's internal read is not counted twice at the call sites.
- Some `B` rows were classified from the reader's docstring and the lines around it, not from a full read of the function.
  The verdict is then the route class, and the column "keys" says what was visible. No row is "unclear": every site fell in a class.
