GIVEN: .claude/settings.json (the hooks block: every event, matcher and command), the hook scripts those commands name (under tools/ and .claude/), docs/FINDINGS.md section F-3.

OWNS (may write): experiments/dev-speed/hook_latency.py (new), experiments/dev-speed/hook-latency.md (new, the result). Nothing else. Temp files go to a temp dir the script creates and removes.

DoD / witness, verbatim in the report: the script's full output table; `git status --short` before and after (identical); `python -B experiments/dev-speed/hook_latency.py --self-check` - a mode that times a no-op command and a `python -c "import time; time.sleep(0.5)"` command and asserts the measured medians are < 300 ms above floor and within 450-900 ms respectively (the negative control that the timer works).
