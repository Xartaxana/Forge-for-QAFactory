- A stage the driver closes by itself is no longer a dead end when an EARLIER stage is still open. The close used to be refused with the controller's "required prior logical stage incomplete", filed as a defect of the factory, and put to the person as a question with `retry` and `stop` only and the line that no word of theirs passes it. Now the driver reads which stage is behind from the controller's one reading of the state and acts. If the earlier stage waits for nobody, the driver goes back to it on its own (one silent retry per controller revision; the same refusal again at the same revision is asked). If the earlier stage waits for its consumer role's acceptance (the failure analyst for the run stage, the doc scribe for the triage stage), the question names that stage and role in plain words and offers `go on`: the person's word is recorded as the acceptance through the same path every such word takes (a driver accept and a finding), and the close runs again. Any other refusal, and a controller state that cannot be read, keep the old question.
- The close of a stage that its consumer role accepts (the run stage, the triage stage) now says who accepted it. The words come from the acceptance record: when a person's word passed the acceptance, the close digest and the question say a person's word accepted it; when the consumer role accepted, the words are the old ones. Before, the close always said the failure analyst or the doc scribe accepted, even when the person's word had.
