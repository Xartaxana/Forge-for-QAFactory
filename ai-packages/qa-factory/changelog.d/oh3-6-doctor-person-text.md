- `qa-doctor` speaks to the person only about what the person does. A readiness remedy whose step names a factory script is run by the doctor itself, or asked as one closed question first and then run; a step only a person can do is phrased as an action, not a command line. Service facts (an unverified context-delivery receipt, activity or `.gitignore` write failures) stand in the closing FACTORY NOTES block, and a report with nothing for the person says so in one line. Two eval cases (16, 17) pin both classes.
