- `rework S1` of a story whose side carries a reference stand now re-enters the S1 reference window: the scout's rework leaves S1 open, the runner walks the old version again and its snapshot is put with `--replace`. This holds on every road that reworks or finishes the S1 scout: the code road, the relaunch road of the person's rework, the ticket-changed rework (it reworks the scout, not the window's runner), and a plain relaunch, a resume or a saved result of the scout (S1 stays open and the next entry runs the window). A resumed role of a survey walk (the reference window, the first look) gets its walk session back on the stand its record names, and the session is closed when the role ends; a walk that never started is not reopened. The runner's reference-window brief names the snapshot contract (`references/artifact-contract-reference.md`). The manifest's merge request (a bare number, `!n`, or a link in any form, leading zeros dropped) is read by ONE reader, `vcs_evidence.mr_iid`, for the MR receipt of the base slice and for the head asked of the remote by the first look. `project_knowledge.py side --deliverable` says when a `--stand` beside it was not used (the answer is the project's); a later word about whose a side is overwrites the value, the earlier row stays as history.
