- Conductor moves are now data columns: each row of `moves` in `conductor-actions.json` gains `args` (`"rework"` for the one move whose option carries the closed `args` list, `null` for every other) and `offerable` (with `not_offered` when false). An option that carries `args` on a move whose column is `null` keeps its place and loses the key (the dropped tokens are noted as `args_dropped` in `state/conductor.jsonl`) instead of making the whole answer unusable. `resume_with_words`, `story_fact` and `revoke_exclusion` are never offerable; their refusal quotes the row's own reason. A column value outside the vocabulary is `DataUnreadable` naming the row.
- When the conductor asked or stopped with a question and its options are refused, the person now reads the conductor's own text instead of only the generic fallback: after the lead, `What the conductor found:` and the text (cut at 1200 characters), then one line for each word it quotes that the shown answers do not carry (`` `restart` is not offered here: ... ``), then the priced fallback options. Gate fallbacks carry the same block and still never offer `go on`.
- The conductor charter shows the option schema without `args` and says only the `rework` option adds it; the moves section of the prompt prints, per open move, whether its option carries `args` and lists the moves that are never options.
