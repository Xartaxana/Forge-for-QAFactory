- A failure rule lifted with `restore_request` is out of what the closing `capture post_action` checks: its result (`requests failed: N`) is written at the lifting, and only a rule still on can refuse the capture. A rule lifted with no match says `surprise: fail_request <glob> matched no request` in the `restore_request` answer and in the first `look` or `read` after it. At the boundary between two items of a walk session every rule the previous item left is lifted and forgotten, its result goes into that item's evidence, and the next item never sees it. A `fail_request` pattern with a single leading `*` (no `**`, no scheme) gets a hint that `*` does not cross `/` (write `**/api/x`); it is a hint, never a refusal. A `press` or `paste` right after a `type` on the same field goes on without a new focus, with the same fact as `type` when the page took the focus away.
