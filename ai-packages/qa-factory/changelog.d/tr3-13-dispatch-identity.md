- A session that works for one dispatch is now told apart from its siblings: the S5 exit checks (the precheck, the branch accounting, the exit gate's digest and scope readers) read "which dispatch is this" through one reader (`dispatch_identity`), from the dispatch id the driver puts in the child's environment or the gate names to its detectors. With two automators running and no dispatch named, the S5 precheck reports it could not tell whose handoff it judges instead of judging every case of the manifest. With one running dispatch nothing changes.
