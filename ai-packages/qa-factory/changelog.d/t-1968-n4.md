- The live probe now records which statement it mutated (`target`: line, end line, the manifest's `assertion_id`, and whether the mutated statement holds that line). A receipt of a probe that broke another line than the obligation's check no longer counts as proof: the window names `semantic_sensitivity` with both doors (probe the Then's line, or return the item `unverified`), delivery does not count the receipt, and a re-probe replaces a stored off-target record. A receipt with no `target`, or with an `assertion_id` that is not of the form `<file>:<line>`, counts as before.
