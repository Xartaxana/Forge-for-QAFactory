- `readiness.py` can now be told the story (`--task <KEY>` or `--task-root <folder>`) and then reads the run's own no-trace record (`pipeline.no_trace`, through the one reader `external_writes.closed_by_run`): a repeated run without `--no-trace` no longer asks the tracker whether it may create issues in an experiment.
