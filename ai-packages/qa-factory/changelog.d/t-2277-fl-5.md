At the start of S3 (before `s3_prepare` and window 1) `Driver.first_look_step` asks `first_look.due`: when no look is due it writes the null record with its reason (a specific reason already written for these heads is kept) and the plan is made as before; when one is due it opens the survey walk for the register's requirements on the story's stand, registers the optional `qa-runner` dispatch of purpose `first_look` named by the S3 row of `optional_dispatches` in `stages.json`, and runs it. The controller no longer refuses that dispatch for lacking an `execution_kind` (one predicate, `controller.runner_kind_required`, for `prepare` and `apply`; every other runner dispatch is refused as before). `story_step.py start` admits a `(role, purpose)` row of `optional_dispatches` without making it a role of the stage, and its `finish` never completes the stage with it; `stage_guard` places the dispatch on its stage and the exit gate judges it by its own output (`work/first-look.yaml`), not by S6's or S3's plan files. Walk actors: the accounts the factory has first (the stand's default login, then the project's roster in its own order), `guest` in a slot left over; an account that exists but was not opened is named to the runner, which records `unreachable` / `access` naming it (`first_look.walk_actors`, one reader for the driver and the runner's brief). A walk that cannot be opened ends the attempt `open_failed` (retried once on the same code; `no_reachable_surface` is not retried), writes the null reason and asks nothing. A dispatch that was not accepted keeps its attempt (a record `put` wrote stays `written`), the plan goes on at once and nothing is asked over it. When the share of requirements the stand showed in an ACCEPTED record is under the floor (`first_look.reachable_floor_percent`, default 30) the closed question `first_look.dead` (`continue` / `stop`, with prices) is asked, once per walk: any answer marks the walk asked (`first_look.mark_asked`); `continue` goes to `controller.py --operation first_look_continue` with the keys of `first_look.py show --json`, `stop` holds the story with cause `first_look_stop` from S3; the machine's `take_answer` now routes the answer to its executor. `first_look.py show --json` also prints `due` and `share`. The exit gate's window-1 test looks only at strategist dispatches that count (`exit_gate.s3_strategist_records`). The S3 phase card carries the same commands for the conductor.
