- When the code critic answers `cannot_judge` on a mutation probe, the person gets their own question at the S5 seam, with the facts: the test, the line and statement of the broken check, the first lines the test printed, and the reviewer's reason. `go on` records one `probe not proved: <case key>` word per case (a new block name, no class: one word never widens); `rework` sends the tests back with the person's words. A `probe gap:` word (instance, case, `tooling:` or class) never names such a test, and a `probe not proved` word older than the judgment is not read. When both kinds are owed in one window, this question comes first and the probe-gap question at the next refusal. A red item, an unjudged record or a never-run probe is never listed.
