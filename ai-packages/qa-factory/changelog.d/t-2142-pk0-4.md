- Project knowledge, phase 0 (PK0-4): gate A asks about an account ONCE per stand and role instead of once per case. When cases of a story sign in as a role the project holds no account for, the gate shows one card for that stand and role (`RA-<stand>-<role>`) listing the cases it holds, with the person's answers and the three price lines per answer: the names of the two variables in the env file (kept for the stand from now on), `accept` (this story only), or "not a role of this product" in their own words; a role whose entries in the stand are ambiguous (twins) gets a card whose door is `myself` or `accept`. A case the person already answered in this story (the question or its own card) is left off the card; the sign-in probe adds a note, not a second card, for an account a card already asks about. A role the strategist noticed and this story's plan names gets a confirm card that does not hold the gate (silence keeps it unconfirmed, and it is asked again next story). An account the person gave in an earlier story is not asked again and is said in one line. No new answer words; the card id is lossless for an ASCII name and a short hash otherwise (a stand name holding a hyphen or a space is always hashed), so two roles or two stands never share one. The card's `the test:` and `missing:` lines are written whole, the machine marker of the "acting on your earlier answers" line never appears in the text a person reads, and a very long stand or role name still leaves the question inside the finding's first 200 characters.
