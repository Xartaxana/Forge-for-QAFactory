- Sides of the product, read from one place. `references/side-realities.json` holds the reality words (`real`, `absent`, `substituted`) and the default side, and `contract.side_realities` is its one reader; `contract.story_sides` returns every side of a story (`reality`, `deliverable`, `settles_by`, `reference`, `source`) from `pipeline.sides`, or the default when the story records none or its file cannot be read (then one line on stderr says why); `contract.parks_after_s5` is true only for an absent side the story delivers; `contract.awaiting` reads the hold of a story waiting for code, with its `cause` and `held_from` (a record written before them reads `s5_absent_side` and `S6`). `stage_done.py --set sides.<name>.<attribute>=<value>` and `--set sides_unknown=` write the record: a reality outside the data file is refused naming the allowed words, a side name outside any list (for example `kiosk`) is accepted, and a stage transition keeps both fields. Nothing writes `pipeline.sides` yet, so every story behaves as before.
