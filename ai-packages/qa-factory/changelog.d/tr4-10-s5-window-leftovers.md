- With parallel windows, S5 no longer leaves test-file claims behind when it completes: once the stage is completed and no S5 dispatch is running, stopped or returned without acceptance, every claim of the stage is freed, whichever finish closed it, so a later rework's automator is not refused over an earlier window's file. A window whose last round came back unaccepted now counts as open, and the serial body of the stage reads the same close rule as the parallel one when more than one window is allowed, so the stage no longer closes over that round. The brief of a window on the serial body now carries the file-ownership line whenever the guard enforces it. With one window nothing changes.
