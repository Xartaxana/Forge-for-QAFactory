- Every execution block now records which sides of the product it ran against. On a story that has a `pipeline.sides` record, a side that is not real, or `pipeline.sides_unknown`, a run block and a manual block carry `sides_met` (side to reality, copied by code at the moment of the run) and, when the sides could not be read, `sides_unknown`; a story with no `pipeline.sides` record, no non-real side and no `sides_unknown` has blocks byte for byte as before. A `pipeline.json` that is present but unreadable (a BOM is read), or a `pipeline.sides` that is not a side record, stamps `sides_unknown` and no `sides_met`, never the default all-real. A reparse copies the stamp of the block it supersedes, an imported run block keeps the stamp it was given at import (the import re-check no longer compares it), and `finalize_task.py` carries it onto every `results[]` and `current_manual_checks[]` row. A walked manual case may carry a record-level `about: [<side>]`, given in the walk verdict or with `manual_evidence.py build --about <side>`; the record check refuses a name that is not a side of the story and names the sides it knows. The qa-runner charter gains one line asking for `about` when the story has a side that is not real.
