- `stand_lease.py`: the stand lease record of an environment now holds `holders: [{job, pid, started, mode, acquired_at}]`. `hold(..., mode="exclusive"|"walk", max_shared=1)`: an `exclusive` holder (a suite, a capture) shares the stand with nobody; up to `max_shared` `walk` holders of distinct jobs (manual walkers) share it with each other and with nothing else. Each holder releases only its own row (job + pid + birth); a holder whose process is gone is dropped at the next acquire and named in the notes; `--release` frees every holder, each named with the person's reason; `--status` lists every holder with its state. `run_suite.hold_stand` passes `mode` and `max_shared` (default exclusive, the suites' call unchanged); a case-lane walk of `walk_session.py` holds the stand in `walk` mode; `probe_debt.stand_state`, the walk's `stand_busy` answer and the onboarding's live-lock listing read the new shape and name every holder. Lease records written by an older package version are not read.
