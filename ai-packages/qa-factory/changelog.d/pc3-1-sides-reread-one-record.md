- The sides re-read after a repaired project registry now records exactly what the first step would have recorded with a readable registry: it merges the registry, the launch words (`--side`, `--reference`) and the person's answers the same way, and the implicit `product` side is decided over that merge, so a launch with `--side ui=real` no longer ends with `product` beside `ui` on one path and without it on the other. A registry that is still unreadable keeps the `sides_unknown` mark and records nothing; an all-real merge writes no side and lifts the mark.
