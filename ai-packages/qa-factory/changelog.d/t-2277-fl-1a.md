- Walk session: a new `survey` place (`walk_session.py open --place survey`) is the first look at a story or a reference stand. Its attempts go under `work/evidence/manual/survey-<item>/aN/`, the stand lease is held for the session's life and no request takes a lease of its own, it has no case verdict (the refusal names `first_look.py put` / `reference_snapshot.py put`), and the close writes the timing. `open` and `serve` take `--env <name>`: the `environments[]` row of that exact name (case counts; an unknown name is refused naming the stands there are) and only that stand's hosts are reachable; without `--env` every existing place walks the first row exactly as before. The walk record now names `place`, `stand`, `profiles` and `attempts`, and the new `capture_cited` returns "" only when a quote is words of a capture file of THIS survey walk's attempt of the item on the named stand, else the reason with its door.
