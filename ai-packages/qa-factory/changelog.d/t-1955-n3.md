- An unprepared manual case now always has an outcome that the runner's own tool writes: `manual_evidence.py unprepared --task-root <root> --case <case> [--observations-file <file>] [--not-walked "<why>"]` records the case with its observations and no pass or fail. Whether the case was walked is derived from the recorder's `steps.jsonl` files, never from the runner's word: a walked case carries its newest attempt (so the recorder's surprise lines travel with it), a case with no recorder step needs `--not-walked` and says why in "Left with you". The runner's prompt line names the command, `write-block` refuses a record with a missing dispatch, time or revision with a door instead of a `KeyError`, the S6 exit refusal names the command, and `unprepared_walk` is a row state in the vocabulary.
