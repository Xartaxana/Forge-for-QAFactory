- The project remembers what a person said about its sides. The answer registry takes three more keys, `product.sides` (whether this team's stories deliver a side, and who makes it real when they do not), `environments.sides` (how a stand holds a side) and `environments.reference_for` (the stand that still runs the old version of a side); side names are compared exactly, so `UI` and `ui` are two sides, and a value outside the shape or a reality word outside `references/side-realities.json` is refused. `project_knowledge.py side` records them on the person's words (closed to roles by the guard, `show` stays open), `project_knowledge.subjects` lists the subjects of a key, and `contract.story_sides` reads the registry after `pipeline.sides` and before the default (each side carries `source: registry` and the `registry_key` it came from). At the start of a story the driver reads these rows, records a person's answer to "whose is this side" once per project so the next story is not asked, refuses a launch side name that differs from a recorded one only by letter case, and writes `pipeline.sides_unknown` when the registry cannot be read or holds a malformed side row; an experiment that leaves no trace keeps the answer in the story only, with one line saying so (the line is also a warning of the story's record). The rows of the stand the story selected are read even when `--env` was not given, a side known only from that stand's row is asked "whose is it", a project that has `product.sides` rows has no implied `product` side, a side name holding a dot or an equals sign is refused (it is a key of the story's record), and a relaunch reads the registry again and clears `sides_unknown` once it reads cleanly.
