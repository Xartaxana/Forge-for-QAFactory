- `probe_by_code.py --plan --task T --instance I --test N [--json]` prints the checks of one test and the runs that would probe them (`{checks, plan, notes}`); it reads, and writes, runs and locks nothing. `checks_of` lists every check of a known form in the test's body, the checks a `then_bindings` row names outside it, and, when the body holds none, the checks of the one helper the hop finds; `run_plan` groups the non-stopping checks into one run and gives a stopping check its own run (or lets it close a group as its last member).
