- The driver's stuck stop no longer says only "I cannot tell from here what is holding it" when an earlier stage is open. When the same step comes back four times in a row, the lead now lists, from the controller state and the pipeline, every earlier stage that is not completed: its status, whether its consumer role's acceptance is awaited or already stands (and for which dispatch), and the controller's reason. A state that cannot be read leaves the old lead, never an invented fact. The count is the named constant `STUCK_REPEATS`.
- A stuck stop the person already answered is not put to them again with the same words. The answer is recorded under the step it was about (`stuck_asked` in `driver-state.json`), and when the same step comes back the text sent to the conductor says what the person answered and when, and that the same question would not move it.
