- New `app_card.py build --task-root <T>` writes `work/app-card.md`, the app card of a story: stand addresses, entry routes (project profile and `work/flow-requests.json` with `path:line`), the sign-in route and whether gate A proved it, account roles and key NAMES, the users the story created (names only), test data, page objects of the repo map and the walkers' notes (`work/app-card-notes.jsonl`). Every section is lines or `not known: <why>`; the first line says the card is no oracle; no credential value is ever printed; the card is at most 8 000 bytes (the entry routes are cut and the tail line names how many); it is rebuilt only when the hash of its inputs changes.
