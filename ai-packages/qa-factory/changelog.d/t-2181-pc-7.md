- The driver now parks a story that waits for code and takes it up again when the code arrives. After S5, when a side the story delivers does not exist yet, the driver writes `interim-report.md` (assembled by code from the sides, the tests per case, what ran and what each run recorded about the sides, and the open placeholders), records the wait (with its cause and the stage it holds from, and the newest accepted S5 dispatch) and stops with exit 0, also under `--until gate_B`. A relaunch with no flag prints the report again and dispatches nothing; a stopped story prints its stopped line and exits 0. A relaunch with `--mr`, `--branch` or `--commit`, or `--side <name>=real` for a recorded side that is not real yet, records the arrival (`implementation_source`, the sides with `source: arrival`, the controller event `arrive`) after the changed-ticket question is asked and before any rework, also on the run that carries the answer to it; the word survives a question asked in the same process, and a replay after a crash records the same sides. The arrival then owes its own rework, one stage per turn after a changed ticket's: the scout (S1) and the requirements tester (S2) go back over their accepted work and the automator runs the bind round (S5), by cause `source_arrived`. A `--side` word for a name the story never recorded is said and changes nothing. The answers of the new questions have executors: `arrival.not_waiting` (`re-ground`, `ignore`, `stop`) and the `not built yet` answer of `s1.no_diff`, which records the product side as absent like its `s0.no_change` twin. `continue_command` repeats `--side` and `--reference`, and the packet's restart line leaves out the `--side <name>=absent` of a side the story waits for. New priced questions `arrival.not_waiting` and `awaiting.stop`.
