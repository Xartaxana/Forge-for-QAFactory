- Collect: a story the person stopped with Ctrl-C is no longer read as a dead driver for the calls that stop cut off. The driver writes one `stopped_by_person` row to `driver-runs.jsonl` (when the process began, and the calls it launched and saw no end for), and the stall rule K3 passes exactly those calls by, naming them as "stopped by the person"; a driver that died on its own, a call of an earlier process, a call launched after the row and a row with no call list are still a stall. The driver's own journals are now appended through the package's one appender, so a torn last line is repaired before the next row; on Windows a new row ends in a bare line feed, not a carriage return and line feed (readers take both).
