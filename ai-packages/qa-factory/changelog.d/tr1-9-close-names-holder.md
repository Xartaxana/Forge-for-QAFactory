- A code-only close of a stage (`controller.py --operation code_only`) no longer waits for a dispatch whose session is over: one the liveness reader finds abandoned or never started does not hold the close, and one that returned but was never accepted does not hold it either. Every dispatch of the closing and earlier stages that stopped and was never accepted is set aside by the receipt (`superseded`, `passed_over_by`), so the story goes on to the next stage; the receipt, the stage entry and one finding per dispatch name it ("returned, never accepted", "abandoned", "stopped, never accepted"), and the report's "Left with you" carries a line for each. A dispatch that is alive or whose state cannot be read (including a reader that fails) still refuses, and the refusal names the dispatch, its role and stage, and the door (wait for it, or release it with the person's word). The driver's question for a close held only by an earlier stage is offered even when a dead dispatch is still marked running.
