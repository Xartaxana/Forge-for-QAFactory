- The report facts now carry counters for a story that has a sides record (`report_facts.py --json`, key `sides_measure`; nothing is added to the report text, and a story with no sides record has no such key): the share of provisional rows settled without touching the marked line (with the count per row status), the cost of the dispatches that ran after code arrived (`phase2_cost`) against the cost of the dispatches made before the first of them (`phase1_cost`; the conductor's own spend is carried apart as `conductor_cost`, and the three share one shape), the plan rows that stand on a substituted or unknown side, the sides that are not real, whether the sides are unknown, and the hold. Each counter is read through the reader that owns its fact (the journal's money as the dashboard reads it, with its per-call-root rule); an input that is absent, unreadable or has no journal record makes the counter `unmeasured: <why>`, never 0, and a story with no phase-2 dispatch prints phase 2 as `absent: <why>`. The architecture map names the side-ledger and awaiting-state tests as detectors of AR-28 and AR-25, and the side readers as carriers of AR-13.
