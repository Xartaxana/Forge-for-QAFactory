- A second `type` on the same field with no step between no longer puts the focus back: it goes on with the typing, as a person does. When the page took the focus away, the keys go where the focus is and the step is not ok (`focus lost before the call: keys went to <what has focus>`, plus a `surprise:` line); a field gone from the page is a failed step with nothing typed. A `click` or any other step between two `type` calls starts the typing afresh. The walk charter says a string typed in a row is ONE `type` call and a recorded focus loss is a finding.
