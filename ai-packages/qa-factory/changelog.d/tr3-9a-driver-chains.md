- The driver's generic chain scheduler moved out of `story_run.py` into a new module `driver_chains.py` (`DriverChains`, the base class of `Driver`; `ChainKind`, `ChainRecord`, `ChainStream`, `Drained`, `once_on`, `ThreadSlot`): no behaviour change. `run_chains` takes two optional keywords, `kind=` (what a chain is called and what its session holds) and `seam=` (the stage's own failure ranking); the S5 console lines, the launch gate and the seam read the same words and files as before, and the module itself names no stage. This is the base the walker lanes of S6 will run on.
