- The first look is no longer skipped as "no stand" because the probe at the start could not log in: `first_look.due` returns `no_stand` only when NO stand is named for the story, and a stand whose probe is pending is walked (the walker is a role and decides whether it can reach the stand; a stand that is really dead still fails at the walk's opening, which is `walk_not_opened`, never "no stand"). The walker's record gains an optional `sign_in` mapping, the way in it found (`surface` ui or http, `actor`, `url`, `steps`, the sign-in `fields` and `landed` rule for a page or an `environment.access`-shaped `access` for requests, `evidence`/`quote`/`req` cited through the walk's own captures, `why`); `first_look.read` returns it as `sign_in` (`{}` for an older record), `put` checks its keys, limits (20 steps of 300 characters, 500 for an address, a selector or a fragment, 20 `url_not` entries), the actor against the walk's actors and the captures, and refuses a text that looks like a secret or holds an account value of the walk by the field's name, never the value. The form `references/artifact-contract-first-look.md` has a new section "The way in you found".
