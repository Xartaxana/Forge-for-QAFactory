- Project knowledge, phase 0 (PK0-1): a new answer registry `scripts/project_knowledge.py` keeps what a person told the factory once about the product - which roles it has, which account a role uses on a stand - in `qa-factory/records/project-knowledge.json` (carried across a reinstall and collected with the other project records), with the person's own words, the scope the answer holds in, who recorded it and when. One writer (`record`, under a lock, refused for a story that leaves no trace) and one pure reader (`state`, config first for an account; an unreadable registry, a revoked row, twins or a malformed note read as unknown and the person is asked). The conductor gets `project_knowledge.py account|role|revoke|show`; `account` writes the stand's account through the existing decision writer first and its record second. Nothing asks differently yet: the stages that read the registry follow in the next nodes.
