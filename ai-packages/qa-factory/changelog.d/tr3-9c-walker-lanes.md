- The manual half of S6 can now run several walkers side by side: the driver puts each lane of manual cases on the same scheduler the S5 windows use, up to `manual.walkers` at a time (default 3; `1` keeps the one-lane-per-entry road exactly as before). A lane does not start while a running lane holds one of its cases or one of its accounts, so two walkers never sign in as the same account and never walk the same case; lanes that share an account run in turn. Lanes that meet a stopped or running dispatch, or whose account cannot be read, run first one at a time and the rest follow side by side. S6 closes only by the finish that leaves no open lane, whatever the order the lanes finish in; an unreadable state counts as open. A lane that crashes lets the others finish and book, and one question or failure is raised after the drain. Known limits: two walkers on different accounts may still touch one entity (isolation comes with the accounts and data way). Guest cases do not wait for each other: each guest lane gets its own guest handle (`guest#<lane>`), so guest lanes run side by side whenever the records they use do not overlap.
