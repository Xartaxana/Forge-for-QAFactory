- Every S7 triage dispatch of the failure analyst (a first one, a scribe-reject rework, a seam rework, a resume) now gets the places to settle and a walk session from one site in the driver: `run_role` appends the place lines before the dispatch's extra lines and opens `walk_session.py open --place settle` only when the role will actually run; the session is closed in a `finally`, so a role that crashes or is refused leaves none behind. A session that cannot open (the stand held or down, the walker absent) is recorded, the brief says "no walk session: <why>" and names the road that needs none (`manual_capture.py ui|http --case settle-<place>`, or `impossible` with that why), and the gap reason of the close follows it. A resumed dispatch whose session is not alive gets it reopened before the words go in. An investigation dispatch gets neither. The analyst may reopen a session that ended with `walk_session.py open --place settle` (a card in the role commands); `walk_session.py verdict` on a settle or control walk is refused with the door `place_answers.py write`. Gate B no longer marks a code-reading draft "by reading the code" once the factory has reproduced it: it shows the reproduction words.
