- A story can now be held for code. The controller keeps one state, `awaiting`, with two causes: `s5_absent_side` (a side the story delivers does not exist yet; held from S6) and `first_look_stop` (the person stopped after the first look; held from S3). While it stands no stage from `held_from` is dispatched, prepared or relaunched (`next` answers `awaiting_implementation`, or the terminal `awaiting_stopped`), a rework of such a stage is refused with its doors, and the stage guard refuses the role's start (a person's recorded word passes it and is journaled as `gate_override`). A rework of the stages before `held_from` runs inside the hold. New controller operations: `await`, `arrive`, `stop_waiting --words-file`, and `first_look_continue --words-file F --source-head H --register-hash R` (the caller's keys are stored as given; it clears a `first_look_stop` hold only, and is refused on `s5_absent_side`, on a stopped story, on empty words, without `H` or with a bad `R`). New purposes `bind`, `reference_walk`, `first_look` and the repair cause `source_arrived` (before `ticket_changed`). `arrive` with nothing held only records the arrival; a stopped story starts no dispatch except the rework the person's words reopen it with (checked where the dispatch commits), and a second `stop_waiting` with the same words after such a reopening is a new decision, not a replay. `stage_guard.py stories` and the archive's open items print the cause and the date; a stopped story is no open item.
