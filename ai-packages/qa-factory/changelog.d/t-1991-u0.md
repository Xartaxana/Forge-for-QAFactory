- The walk session has two lanes for the failure analyst (`walk_session.py open --place case|settle|control`, default `case` = unchanged). A `settle` session opens its attempts under the same root as `manual_capture.py ui|http --case settle-<place id>` (`work/evidence/manual/settle-<id>/aN/`); a `control` session under `evidence/control-<id>/aN/`, the folder a triage row's `control.evidence` is checked in. Both take the stand lease per request (waiting at most `request_lease_wait_s`, 60 s, then exit 3 `stand_busy` naming the holder) instead of for the session's life, so the analyst's own test runs are never blocked by its session, and are not ended by `idle_s` (they end with their dispatch, their parent or `max_s`). An item id with a slash, a drive, `..` or `__` is refused naming `[A-Za-z0-9._-]+`; an unknown `--place` is refused listing the three.
