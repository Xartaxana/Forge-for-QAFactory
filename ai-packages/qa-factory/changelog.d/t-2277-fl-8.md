The keys are present only for a story with a first look or a registry fact it knew before it began. The retro now prints the question count with its counter-metrics and one table of reached / used / not used, with the finding lines "the first-look record was not read" and "a known fact did not reach a decision"; a count of zero questions is printed only with its counter-metrics. Counters never refuse and never judge how well a fact was used.
